1. Scope and the Company's role

  1. This Policy applies to information about corporate-customer contacts, administrators, operators, people who contact the Company, and facility visitors whose information may be collected while equipment operates.
  2. The Company distinguishes information it collects for account administration or inquiries from information entrusted by corporate customers for equipment operations. Entrusted data is handled within applicable law, contract terms, and lawful instructions.
  3. For information a corporate customer independently collects or uses, please also review that customer's notices and policies. This clause does not transfer to customers duties that legally apply to the Company.

2. Information collected

Depending on the features used, device model and settings, contract, and permissions granted on the device, the Service may collect the following information, limited to what is needed for the features actually provided.

  • Corporate and account information: company and contact names, business contact details, user names, phone numbers, email addresses, authentication information, affiliation, roles, permissions, and change records.
  • Device and operation information: model, serial number and other identifiers such as MAC address, firmware and app versions, operating and fault status, battery and consumables, tasks, cleaning area and duration, device location within a facility, maps, and routes.
  • Environmental data: spatial and obstacle data from supported LiDAR, depth, ultrasonic, infrared, and similar sensors. Image and video data is addressed in Section 4.
  • Operation and network information: login, operation, command-delivery and error records, access time, IP address, network type, and device or app information needed to diagnose connectivity and faults.
  • Inquiry information: inquiry contents, contact details, response history, and related materials voluntarily provided by the customer.

Robot location data is used to manage equipment inside a facility, not to track visitors' smartphone movements. Do not enter or send sensitive personal information, such as medical information, unless necessary; any necessary handling will be addressed separately in accordance with law.

3. Smartphone permissions

The current app does not access the smartphone camera, photo library, or microphone. These are separate from sensors and cameras installed on the robot.

4. Robot images, video, and public facilities

  1. Images or video are collected, viewed, transmitted, or stored only when the relevant feature is offered on a compatible robot with the necessary settings. Before offering or adding such a feature, we explain what is collected, why, where it is sent, who may view it, and whether and how long it is retained, and obtain any consent required by law.
  2. If an image contains a face or other information that identifies a person, it is handled as personal information even when identifying that person is not the purpose. Facility maps and operating logs are also protected according to their nature when they contain personal or confidential information.
  3. The Service is not intended for facial identification or profiling people's behavior. Images are used only as necessary for safety control, obstacle avoidance, route planning, anomaly checks, and equipment management authorized by law and the customer.
  4. The Company and corporate customers confirm their respective roles and, according to the features provided, give notices required by law, limit collection, restrict viewing permissions, and delete unnecessary information. Feature-specific safeguards and retention conditions are disclosed with the feature.

5. Purposes of use

  • Creating and authenticating accounts, managing permissions, registering customers and equipment, and administering contracts.
  • Connecting to equipment, remote management, task and map management, operation monitoring, safety control, and providing the Service.
  • Diagnosing faults, responding to inquiries and maintenance requests, updating software, and improving quality.
  • Managing access, preventing unauthorized use, diagnosing faults, conducting security audits, and responding to incidents.
  • Communicating as needed about contracts, fees, maintenance, important changes, outages, and safety.
  • Meeting legal obligations and appropriately handling disputes and claims.

Service improvement is limited to a scope reasonably related to these purposes and permitted by contract. This Policy does not provide blanket permission to use personal information to train AI models, deliver personalized advertising, or support unrelated services. If additional use is needed, we will explain it and complete required procedures.

6. Collection, consent, and changes of purpose

The Company obtains information from user input, transmissions from supported equipment, app or system use, inquiries, or provision by a corporate customer with lawful authority. We notify or publish purposes of use and obtain consent in advance when required by law, including for sensitive personal information.

If purposes change, the change will remain within a reasonably related scope and the revised purpose will be notified or published. Uses beyond that scope will be made only after required consent or other procedures, except where a statutory exception applies.

7. Service providers and disclosure to third parties

  1. The Company does not sell personal information. Personal data is provided to a third party only with consent or another legal basis. Inquiries or fault response do not authorize unrestricted disclosure to unrelated parties.
  2. When processing is entrusted for service delivery, equipment maintenance, communications, or operational support, it is limited to what is necessary. The Company selects providers, requires contractual safeguards, and provides necessary supervision. If a provider or equipment maker uses data for its own purposes, that use is distinguished from entrusted processing and separately explained, with consent where required.
  3. Information may be provided within the scope of legal exceptions, such as when required by law or necessary to protect life, body, or property and obtaining consent is difficult. A request from a public authority alone does not automatically authorize disclosure.
  4. When information is transferred through a merger or business succession, applicable law is followed and continuity of the purposes and safeguards is ensured. Before starting joint use, the Company will notify or publish its scope, users, purposes, and responsible manager.
  5. Where legal confirmation or record-keeping duties for third-party provision apply, the Company follows them. Information about providers and external services is supplied with relevant feature information or in response to inquiries, and beforehand whenever law requires.

8. Storage location and access from outside Japan

  1. The Service's primary servers are located in the Company's self-operated data center in Saitama Prefecture, Japan. Data on those primary servers is stored and operated in Japan.
  2. If maintenance access from outside Japan, transmission to equipment manufacturers, or handling by an overseas provider occurs, the Company provides information required by law about the countries or regions, parties, purposes, and safeguards, and completes the required procedures. Locating primary servers in Japan does not remove those requirements.

For transfers to a third party in a foreign country, the Company completes procedures required by Japanese law. If consent is the basis, we provide legally required information about the destination country, its data-protection system, and the recipient's safeguards before obtaining consent. If continuing equivalent safeguards are the basis, we make required confirmations, take necessary measures, and provide information to the individual.

9. Retention and deletion

Information is kept only as long as needed, considering the purpose, contract, nature of the information, statutory retention duties, and dispute handling. Information no longer needed is appropriately erased or processed into statistics that do not identify individuals. Replacing an identifier alone does not automatically make information anonymous.

Requests for deletion may be made under Section 11; information that must be retained after a contract ends is restricted to that purpose.

10. Security and incident response

The Company implements safeguards appropriate to the risks, including management responsibility and internal rules, employee training and supervision, access controls for systems and facilities, authentication and permission management, communications protection, log review, and provider oversight.

If a leak, loss, or damage occurs, the Company verifies the facts, limits harm, investigates causes, and prevents recurrence. Where required by law, it reports to the Personal Information Protection Commission and notifies affected individuals. Inquiries about safeguards may be sent to the office in Section 15. Details that could compromise security may be withheld to the extent permitted by law.

11. Requests to exercise rights

An individual or lawful representative may, within the scope permitted by law, request notice of purposes, disclosure, correction, addition or deletion, suspension or erasure, suspension of third-party provision, or disclosure of third-party provision records for personal data the Company is authorized to handle. Requests are reviewed under applicable requirements and are not automatically granted in every case.

Send a request to kobun.ma@inclo.co.jp with the subject "Personal Information Request" and include the relevant account, requested action, and contact details. The Company performs only the identity or authority checks reasonably necessary and responds without undue delay as required by law. Identity documents should be submitted only when necessary and through the secure procedure we provide.

We explain the requested disclosure method, any fee and its amount, and the procedure before processing. If a request cannot be granted or the method must change, we explain why as required by law. We do not impose unreasonable conditions that obstruct statutory rights.

For data entrusted by a corporate customer where the Company has no independent authority to respond, we will cooperate by directing the request to or coordinating with the authorized customer. Deleting an app account and ending a corporate customer's contract are separate procedures.

12. External services and SDKs

If an external service or SDK is incorporated into the app, information appropriate to the feature will be provided, including its name, provider, purpose, transmitted items, destination and country of handling, privacy policy, and relevant settings or opt-out method. If advance notice, explanation, or consent is legally required, the necessary steps are completed before that handling begins.

Please also review the external service's own notices and privacy policy. Processing entrusted by the Company or disclosure to third parties is governed by Sections 7 and 8.

13. Minors and facility visitors

The Service is not intended for minors. However, when equipment operates in a public facility, information about visitors, including minors, may be collected. The Company and corporate customers take account of necessity, notice, and access controls in the circumstances. Where the law requires consent from a parent or guardian, appropriate procedures are followed.

14. Changes to this Policy

The Company revises this Policy when laws, features, or actual information handling changes. Revisions and their effective dates are announced in the app, by email, or by another appropriate method. Important changes are announced with a reasonable advance period. When new consent is required, posting the revision or continued use alone is not treated as consent; the required consent or other procedure is obtained.
